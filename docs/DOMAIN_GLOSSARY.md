# Domain Glossary

Status: draft.

## Invoice

A request for payment issued by Northstar Beverages to a fictional retailer. It includes invoice number, purchase order reference, issue date, due date, line items, total amount, and open balance.

## Payment

Money received from a retailer. A payment may settle one invoice, partially settle an invoice, or cover multiple invoices.

## Remittance

Evidence sent with a payment that explains which invoices the payment applies to and whether any deductions were taken.

## Deduction

The difference between the invoice amount due and the payment amount received for a matched invoice or allocation. A deduction can be validated, unexplained, disputed, or routed to review.

## Promotion

A pre-authorized trade promotion, allowance, discount, or agreement that can justify some or all of a deduction if the retailer, date window, reference, and amount constraints match.

## Allocation

The assignment of all or part of a payment to one or more invoices. Allocations must not exceed the payment amount or the eligible invoice open amount.

## Review Task

A human work item created when the system cannot safely confirm an extraction, match, deduction, or state transition.

## Audit Event

An immutable record of a workflow-changing action, including actor, action, target entity, timestamp, correlation identifier, and before/after details where appropriate.
